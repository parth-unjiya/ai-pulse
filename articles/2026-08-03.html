    3EU Introduces AI Act with New Transparency Rules (The Verge)

    The European Union has introduced the AI Act, which includes new transparency rules requiring companies to disclose interactions with chatbots and AI deepfakes online. This regulation is crucial because it addresses the growing concern about the lack of transparency in AI decision-making. The AI Act will have far-reaching implications for companies operating in the EU, as they will need to adapt to the new regulations and ensure that their AI systems are transparent and explainable. This development will likely influence AI regulation globally, as other countries consider similar measures to ensure accountability and trust in AI systems.
